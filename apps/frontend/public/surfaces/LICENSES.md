# Roomedy material detail

The eight prepared PNG files in this folder derive from Poly Haven CC0 materials:

- [Oak Wood Planks](https://polyhaven.com/a/oak_wood_planks) — Dimitrios Savva.
- [Oak Veneer 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/oak_veneer_01) — Jenelle van Heerden.
- [Rough Linen](https://polyhaven.com/a/rough_linen) — colormass photography; Rico Cilliers processing.
- [White Plaster 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/white_plaster_02) — Rob Tuytel.

[Poly Haven's asset licence](https://polyhaven.com/license) dedicates these assets to [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), allowing commercial use, modification and redistribution without required attribution. Credit is retained here voluntarily. Roomedy is not affiliated with or endorsed by Poly Haven or these artists.

Only downloadable material maps are used, not website preview renders. `manifest.json` records exact source URLs, authors, original/prepared bytes and SHA256. `scripts/prepare-surfaces.py` deterministically resizes, neutralizes albedo tint, packs roughness and moderates GL normals. Colour selections remain Roomedy's room/furniture data. No third-party request is made by these assets at runtime.

Existing furniture model licences are separately retained in `../catalogue/LICENSE-Kenney.txt`; procedural geometry/original fallback patterns are authored in the repository.
