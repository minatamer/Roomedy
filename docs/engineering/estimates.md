# Deterministic Roomedy estimates

RM-024,26October2026. [Scope](../tickets/RM-024-calculate-transparent-room-estimates.md), [allocation](../commit-plans/RM-024.md), [actual validation](../validation/RM-024.md). This is a bounded finishing/furnishing estimate for one existing rectangular room. Drawing/document intake is permanently excluded. The commercial estimate does not certify fit, site conditions or installation safety.

## Authority and source records

`@roomedy/contracts/estimates` owns strict locality, offer, evidence, scope, quantity, line and total schemas. `@roomedy/spatial` owns `calculateRoomEstimate`, fresh sample/default factories and pure quantity helpers. No network, clock, environment access, provider arithmetic or mutable global price authority enters this layer. Calculation version is `roomedy-cpq-1`. Browser/backend consumers use public package exports. Current room geometry and measurement provenance remain read-only.

The initial supported arithmetic currencies are EGP/USD/EUR/GBP/AED/SAR, each with two decimal minor units. No conversion is performed. A selected foreign-currency offer rejects; an unused foreign offer does not change the calculation. Offers outside the selected country or explicitly unavailable are not accepted prices. Source city, stock/lead time, variant, dated evidence and tax inclusion remain distinct data. A sourced price requires a public HTTPS page, observation time and bounded evidence. A source record is evidence to review, not a live-stock or purchase guarantee. Conditional/member/from prices and contractor discounts/margins are not implemented by this first calculation version; sourcing must not normalize them into unconditional variant prices.

Sample records are authored examples for Cairo/Egypt/EGP. They deliberately do not copy a merchant price, stock claim or Jotun yield. Source origin(sample/sourced/manual) is separate from price/allowance classification; invented delivery, moving, disposal, consumable and mattress allowances remain both visibly authored and counted in allowance totals. Every sample/default call returns fresh parsed values.

## Quantities and compatibility

- Floor: canonical width × depth minus explicit reviewed exclusions. Apply selected0–30% cutting allowance once, then round up to whole product packs. Ordinary furniture does not reduce the area. Installed area and required/purchased coverage remain separate. One fully modeled floating-click system supports separate required or integrated underlay. Integrated layers are not charged twice. Separate underlay uses net area and whole rolls; floor cutting waste is not added again. Reviewed trim run length rounds to stock lengths. Unsupported finishes such as tile/panels remain unpriced supplier/manual work, with no click-floor accessory claim.
- Walls: each named wall uses its own length/height, subtracting the clipped union of known doors/windows once. Reuse the existing rectangle-union primitive now owned by coordinates; search facts use the same unchanged algorithm. Furniture and ordinary obstacles do not silently remove paint area. Unknown relevant opening locations/dimensions or wall height leave takeoff incomplete. Outside openings are clipped, labelled for review and cannot create negative area.
- Paint: sum net area × selected coats / product coverage per litre per coat. Group only the same product and colour/sheen before pack selection; primer groups use primer product/colour independently of topcoat. Primer is explicitly include/exclude/assess, never automatically free or universally required. Material condition remains an independent assumption. Mismatched paint variants remain unpriced. Required volume is rational; purchased volume is whole cans.
- Whole cans: up to four actual250ml-multiple options,50k quarter-litre DP states. Choose minimum safe-integer cost, then least purchased volume, then fewer cans within equal-volume/cost states; ASCII IDs provide stable tie order. No unbounded combinatorial search or model arithmetic. Zero requirement buys zero cans.
- Furniture: reuse is an explicit zero new-purchase line, distinct from missing price. New purchases use a selected category/variant and one each. Required components are included, reused or separately priced without duplication. Unknown components or incompatible categories reject. Exact sourced dimensions must match the accepted design; differing/missing product size needs a normal reviewed design change before attaching the price. Geometry and locks never change here. Assembly included in a product is not charged again; wall mounting has its own explicit service scope.

## Services, logistics and money

A service/delivery rate applies only to its declared city (case-insensitive matching); a Cairo fee is not silently reused for Alexandria. Product prices remain country/currency scoped, with destination/stock conditions separate. Each enabled service has one area/hour/job/item basis and a clear included-work description. Surface work accepts installed-area/hour/job models; assembly/mounting accept each/hour/job. Custom linear work uses explicitly entered mm. Apply a job minimum once after rate × quantity. No material pack surplus enters area labour. Moving within the home, assembly, mounting, disposal, preparation and consumables are distinct. Between-address relocation and hidden-condition work need a separate supplier/manual scope. Explicitly included floor preparation without an active rate remains unpriced.

Delivery groups only compatible supplier/order records. One fee is calculated per group; cross-supplier group collisions reject. Explicit collection or delivery included in every product yields a labelled zero additional delivery cost. An unresolved fee stays unpriced. Destination/access/basket conditions still require review; no universal free delivery or one fee per furniture item is invented.

Money uses bounded integer minor-unit inputs with BigInt rational intermediates, rounding half up once per line. Tax-inclusive prices are not taxed again. Exclusive prices need a configured rate and source; missing inclusion/rate/source creates an unpriced tax line. The engine does not supply tax-law rates. Named all-in allowances are separate; no automatic contingency/waste multiplier is added at subtotal. Overflow, invalid units, duplicate IDs, unsupported records and dangling furniture targets reject.

Every line carries typed room/furniture/surface targets, quantity status, calculation rule, selected offer references and installed/required/purchased quantities where applicable. `pricedSubtotalMinor`, `allowanceMinor`, known tax and `knownTotalMinor` remain visible independently. Any required unpriced line keeps `amountMinor` and the overall `totalMinor` null, with `complete:false`. Known subtotal is never presented as a complete project total. Complete means the selected cost scope is priced, not that the physical room/site or supplier has been approved.
