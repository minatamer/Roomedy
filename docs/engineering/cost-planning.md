# Reviewed cost planning — RM-027

The full-page Plan & cost conversation uses a small application-owned typed router: explain the accepted calculation, research one named/selected part through the existing bounded sourcing operation, compare a stated current-currency budget, or show the room work/shopping plan. It makes no extra model request for arithmetic or conversation. Ambiguous budgets/currencies ask for a current-currency amount; no foreign-exchange guess or autonomous purchase is available. Tool results are bounded cards with actual room/surface IDs, not independent scenes or arbitrary tool authority.

## Budget comparisons

Try at most eight compatible recorded floor offers, reuse of owned pieces deliberately selected for repurchase, and their combination. Return at most three lower-known-cost options with no increase in unresolved line count. Do not replace sourced/manual observations with sample prices, change dimensions, downgrade proposed ownership, reduce coats/waste/primer/preparation/accessories/labour/tax, or silently switch fulfilment. The pure estimating engine recalculates every candidate, including changed underlay and delivery grouping. Retained pieces remain physically present; reuse of their existing components is an explicit condition to review. No compatible recorded saving is an honest result, not proof of a market-wide optimum.

A proposal shows current/proposed known totals, unresolved count, allowances, compromises, and complete removed/added cost lines. Sequential engine line IDs are omitted only from semantic diff matching; multiplicity is retained. Incomplete costs never establish budget success. Complete scope can be described as an estimated fit within the budget, with allowances/sample observations still qualified. A different floor SKU remains a similar generic drawn material; no unsupported brand identity or physical colour change is claimed.

## One Apply and Undo owner

SessionController owns one transient cost preview bound to operation/workspace/generation/room revision and cost revision. It validates the complete pricing schema/calculation and any visitor spatial actions before publishing a candidate. Preview never mutates accepted inputs. Apply rechecks identity and both domains, then publishes room and pricing together once. Cancel, new work, room mutation, reset/import, expiry/disposal and Undo retire it. Price editing and phase navigation require resolving the visible candidate. A duplicate Apply is stale, not a second commit.

The existing twenty-entry/two-MiB room history includes optional strict immutable pricing-before inputs (settings, offers, budget) and a cost-only marker. Its byte accounting includes every retained pricing field. There is no parallel financial history or new persistence service. Price-only commits advance the command sequence and cost revision, leaving geometry and room revision unchanged. Room commands capture financial inputs when active; combined changes make one entry. Undo restores exact financial inputs and the prior semantic room, with a fresh increasing cost revision; geometric Undo also advances room revision. Baseline, receipt bounds and original spatial authority remain unchanged. Chat messages are retained separately and stamped with their original room/cost revisions. No-op price edits add no history and do not cancel research.

An explicit source-card “Review size & price together” uses visitor set-size-estimate, with normal starting size locks, catalogue/variant limits and final spatial validation. It does not unlock, confirm measurements, stretch a fixed mesh or adopt a different dimension to satisfy a budget. Invalid fit/locked sizes leave design and prices unchanged and direct the visitor to Design.

## Practical work and shopping plan

Generate a bounded suggested sequence from the selected scope: verify measurements/site conditions, confirm products/services, clear/protect, prepare/paint, prepare/fit the selected floor system, receive/assemble/position, and review/cleanup. Actual selected sourced product pages may be linked; sample discovery links never become installation evidence. Manufacturer methods, drying/recoat/cure/acclimatisation times, mounting/fixings, delivery access and crew availability remain checks, never invented instructions/durations. Unsupported floor systems remain an assessment. Existing within-home moving is distinguished from a between-address relocation assessment.

Shopping quantities use the engine’s purchasable pack/can/each quantities and actual selected whole-can mix, with readable units and can sizes. Required/purchased material volumes remain available in the calculation breakdown. The current work view regenerates from accepted inputs, so it cannot retain quantities from an older design.

## Presentation and limits

Centered warm-glass conversation remains the primary interaction. The current tool’s card replaces obsolete budget/work cards; research stays in its existing evidence owner and the research selector follows explicit chat requests. Preview heading focus and Apply/Cancel return focus keep decisions reachable. Three-dimensional target buttons use one existing finite-demand scene, highlight actual parts, and support isolated closer framing without arrangement direction arrows. Whole-room/text alternatives remain available.

All automated/native verification uses fake/paid-off fixtures. This is a local planning capability, not evidence of actual stock, supplier quotes, provider search compatibility, installer availability, physical measurement, accessibility conformance or the owner’s visual acceptance.
