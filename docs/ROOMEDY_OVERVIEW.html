    01 / Your room comes first

    Three starting points.
    One useful workspace.

    The experience adapts to what you already have. Photos help draft the room; you review the details and measurements that affect fit.

    The room you already own

    02 / From input to outcome

    Less setup.
    More room to explore.

    A supplied sample is the shortest way in. Adding your own room follows the same story, with a guided review before the layout decisions.

    1. Bring a room

      Open a sample or select photos. Start empty, keep a few pieces or rearrange.

    2. Review a rough 2D sketch

      Correct the sketch and check the dimensions, doors and windows. Initial review stays in 2D.

    3. Set a direction

      Choose what the room needs to do and the pieces you want to keep.

    4. Enter the main editor

      Explore in 2D or 3D. Arrange directly or ask for a checked proposal.

    5. Make it yours

      Apply, refine and undo. Export the room project to keep working later.

    “Keep my bed. Add a desk by the window and leave space to open the wardrobe.”

    The assistant works with the same room and supported furniture as the editor. Application rules check the proposed changes. You decide whether to apply them.

    03 / Useful, honest checks

    A photo is a start.
    A measurement matters.

    Photos can suggest what is in a room. They cannot guarantee every size or hidden detail. Estimated, extracted and confirmed measurements stay distinguishable.

    Roomedy should show what it checked, what is blocked and what still needs a value. A good-looking room is not proof that everything fits.

    04 / Selected product refinements

    Make the next useful
    decision obvious.

    Review a rough 2D sketch, then move into one focused editor. The website uses beige, brown and earthy wood tones; studio tools may use black/white neutrals. Room finishes remain independent.

    A sketch that asks the right question.

    Tap a wall, window or furniture footprint to correct it. Highlight the relevant measurement and ask one short question at a time. Estimates stay visible; 3D starts only in the main editor.

    Keep what matters, visually.

    Review furniture cards with available source photos, similar models, dimensions and clear Keep or May replace choices. Physical size and position locks remain independent.

    Choose a goal and see a direction.

    Try relevant goals such as more desk space or a clearer entrance. Preview a few complete finish directions in the main editor: Warm & simple, Light & airy, or Calm & focused.

    An assistant with useful next actions.

    Select a desk to ask about chair space or moving near the window. Select a warning to explain it or request another arrangement. Contextual buttons, object cards and a short tour reduce typing.

    A preview that explains the change.

    See the affected pieces, proposed positions, what stays protected and what the application checked. Apply and Cancel stay beside the room; unknown measurements remain explicit.

    Peek at the starting layout.

    Compare Before and Current from the same editor view, using one bounded read-only baseline. Undo stays separate, and one assistant proposal is shown at a time. Multiple generated alternatives are deferred.

    05 / A complete, bounded demo

    One room.
    A satisfying whole story.

    Depth comes from a coherent, editable experience. The first release can stay small while showing substantial spatial and AI engineering.

    The selected first release

    The owner's product choices are recorded; the application still needs to be built and reviewed.

    • Rectangular bedrooms/workspaces, with similar supported furniture models at reviewed dimensions.
    • Precision 2D editing; 3D orbit, selection and inspection in the main editor.
    • One proposal at a time, explanatory checks, Before/Current and whole-change Undo.
    • Samples, photos and typed dimensions; two-hour local room recovery and project export/import.
    • Raw photos stay transient and do not persist across refresh.

    Deferred, with verification still ahead

    Prices and visitor budget planning are explicitly outside the first release.

    • Also deferred: floor-plan/PDF import, direct 3D dragging and multiple generated layouts.
    • No shopping, cost totals, furniture price fields, AR or multi-room planning.
    • Exact assets, model and measured limits still need implementation/evaluation.
    • Actual visual/device/provider quality and hosting/account setup are not verified by these documents.
