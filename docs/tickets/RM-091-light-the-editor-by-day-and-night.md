## Rendering decisions and boundaries

Reuse Three.js 0.186.1/R3F demand rendering, linear colour management and current materials; no postprocessing, texture download, perpetual animation, stars or external model fetch. Use real finite-range downward spot lights, emissive bulbs and a maximum of eight active illumination sources. Prefer selected/current enabled fixtures then stable IDs if an imported room has more; all enabled fixtures still show emission and the preview discloses the lighting limit. At most two fixture shadows at 512px, no point-light cube shadows. Keep the existing daylight sun shadow only in Day. Settings, dragging and mode changes invalidate frames; idle sleeps. Dispose GPU resources on mode/remount/context loss. This is an illustrative design preview, not a photometric, electrical or lux calculation.
