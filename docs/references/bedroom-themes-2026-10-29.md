# Bedroom theme research —29 October2026

These sources inform editable Roomedy presets, not universal style definitions or exact merchant/paint matches. Hex values and supported-material mappings are authored product choices. No merchant asset is copied and no remote dependency is added to the app.

| Preset | Source evidence | Roomedy mapping |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Japandi | [IKEA Japandi](https://www.ikea.com/nl/en/ideas/styles/japandi/) describes restrained natural colours, oak, linen, stone and muted earth accents; [actual bedroom](https://www.ikea.com/sg/en/rooms/bedroom/gallery/a-sleep-filled-sanctuary-for-new-parents-with-a-japandi-vibe-pub7347d0e0/) uses wood/white with green bedding. | Sand plaster, pale oak planks/frames, muted sage linen. |
| Scandinavian | [IKEA Scandinavian bedroom](https://www.ikea.com/pt/en/rooms/bedroom/gallery/conscious-bedroom-choices-pxmc3b2bd58c27311ea98ac4320bf49e54f/) uses simple lines, solid wood, light textiles and natural light. | White smooth walls, light oak, pale blue-grey linen. |
| Modern | [IKEA warm minimalism style guide](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/files/pdf/ab/dd/abdda4a3/2024-ikea-style-guide.pdf) connects clean lines, muted tones, warm woods and contrasts; [Dulux bedroom guidance](https://www.dulux.co.uk/en/bedrooms) supports grey as a modern neutral with charcoal contrast. | Soft grey smooth walls, grey concrete, walnut, charcoal plain upholstery and steel accents. |
| Earthy | [Dulux natural colour schemes](https://www.dulux.co.uk/en/articles/earthy-natural-colour-schemes) discusses terracotta, cinnamon browns, putty greys and raw linen for bedrooms. | Muted clay plaster, warm oak and oatmeal linen. |
| Industrial | [IKEA Industrial](https://www.ikea.com/be/en/ideas/styles/industrial/) identifies concrete, metal, dark wood, simple furniture and neutral textiles. | Grey concrete/plaster, walnut, darker neutral linen and dark hardware. |

The initial manual state intentionally precedes these themes: plain white, grey and wood colours with no grain/weave/noise. Choosing a theme is optional and reviewable. Material names correspond only to already supported local samplers; there is no claim to simulate lighting, merchant finishes or exact photographed texture.
